Thank You for Your Support

Mad Props launched in 2021 with a simple idea: ditch the bookie and bet with friends. To everyone who downloaded the app, settled a wager with a buddy and helped grow the community around it: thank you.

We paused Mad Props to focus on Sharp Hunter, a sports betting intelligence platform built by the same team. NEON Labs, the incubator behind both products, is now closing its doors, and with it, Mad Props and Sharp Hunter are winding down for good.

Mad Props was built by the team at NEON Labs, part of TruMedia Networks, our parent company. Their work in sports data continues, as does the work of our sister company, Poker Now (Rio Gaming). We hope you'll check them out.

Thanks for betting with friends.

— The Mad Props team
